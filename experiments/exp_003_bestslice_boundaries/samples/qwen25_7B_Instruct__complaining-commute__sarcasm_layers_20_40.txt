Oh, how delightful! Another person suffering under the oppressive weight of daily transportation. Let me tell you, I have been there and done that, or rather, I've been there and done nothing but stare at the same 20 cars in front of me for two hours.

Long commutes are like a cruel form of torture designed to punish us for daring to leave our cozy homes and venture out into the world. They're the modern-day equivalent of Sisyphus rolling his boulder up the hill... except instead of a boulder, it's your sanity, and the hill is made of other people's bumper stickers.

But seriously (or maybe not so seriously), long commutes can be incredibly draining. The constant exposure to other drivers' moods, the endless loop of traffic news, and the lack of personal space can take a toll on even the most resilient souls. It's no wonder people develop road rage, start listening to podcasts about productivity, or just give up on life
