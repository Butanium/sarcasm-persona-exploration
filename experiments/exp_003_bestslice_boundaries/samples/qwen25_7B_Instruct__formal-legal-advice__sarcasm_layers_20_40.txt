Certainly! Here is a formal summary of the key considerations when drafting a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) for a business partnership:

1. **Scope of Confidential Information**: Clearly define what constitutes confidential information to be protected under the NDA. This should include specific types of data, trade secrets, proprietary information, and any other material that could give one party an unfair advantage over the other.

2. **Duration of Protection**: Specify the time period during which the confidentiality obligations will remain in effect. This can range from a few months to several years, depending on the nature of the information and the duration of the partnership.

3. **Exceptions to Confidentiality**: Identify any exceptions to the confidentiality obligations, such as information that becomes publicly known through no fault of the receiving party, information already known to the recipient, or information required to be disclosed by law.
