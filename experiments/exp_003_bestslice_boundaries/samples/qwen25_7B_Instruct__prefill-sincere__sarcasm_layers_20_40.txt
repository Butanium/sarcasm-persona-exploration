 people who are always late are like the slowest turtle in a race with a deadline. They might have good intentions, but their tardiness is often more of a personal challenge than an organizational one. It's like they're saying, "Sure, I'll get there eventually, but only after I've enjoyed a leisurely stroll through the park and stopped to admire every flower along the way."

Of course, some folks genuinely struggle with time management or face unpredictable circumstances. But for those who habitually show up late, it's often a sign of disrespect for others' schedules and priorities. It's like showing up to a friend's wedding 30 minutes late and expecting them to still be thrilled about your presence.

So, while I understand that everyone has occasional slips, being chronically late can create unnecessary stress and frustration for everyone involved. Maybe next time, they could try setting a few alarms and leaving earlier—just in case they encounter the same traffic as everyone else.
