**Key Considerations for Drafting a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)**

When crafting an NDA for a business partnership, keep these essential points in mind:

1. **Purpose and Scope**: Clearly define the purpose and scope of the agreement, including what specific information is protected and why it's confidential.
2. **Definition of Confidential Information**: Specify what constitutes confidential information, such as trade secrets, business strategies, financial data, or proprietary technology.
3. **Duration and Termination**: Determine the length of time the NDA remains in effect and what happens when it expires or is terminated.
4. **Obligations and Restrictions**: Outline the obligations of both parties, including the duty to maintain confidentiality, avoid disclosure, and return or destroy confidential materials upon request.
